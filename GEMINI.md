# ReadingRate

The `readingrate` MCP server is ReadingRate's book catalog and reading tracker (https://readingrate.com).

- Book facts such as difficulty, reading time, literary regard, prizes and ranked lists come from its tools. Quote them rather than estimating.
- The catalog works without an account. The reader's library, reading log, quotes, notes and book clubs need a ReadingRate account. When a tool says the reader is not signed in, tell them to run `/mcp auth readingrate`, which opens ReadingRate's sign-in in their browser.
- The skills in this extension cover the common requests: logging reading from a sentence, choosing the next book, where to start with a literature, a shelf photo, a debrief after a finished book, and book club prep.
